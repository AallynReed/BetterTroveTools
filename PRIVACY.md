# Privacy Policy

Better Trove Tools ("BTT") is a free, fan-made companion app for the game Trove, made by Aallyn Reed. This policy covers the BTT Android app.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

## What stays on your device

Your settings, favourites and rotation reminders are stored only on your phone. Reminders are scheduled locally by Android; no push notification service is involved. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

BTT has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## What the app downloads

To show game data, images, mods and updates, the app requests content from:

- `api.aallyn.net` and `trove.aallyn.net` (BTT's own service)
- `trovesaurus.com` (game data and item images)
- `api.github.com` (release notes and update checks)
- `fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com` (fonts)

Like any website, these servers receive your IP address and basic request details. BTT does not use that to identify you or build a profile.

## Feedback you send

If you use the in-app feedback form, BTT receives the message, its category, the app version, any files you attach and any contact details you choose to enter. It is used only to answer you and fix problems, is never sold or shared, and is deleted on request.

## Children

BTT is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@aallyn.net, or the BTT Discord.
